Frequently Asked Questions

How much is the deposit?

The deposit is $500 and is applied toward the $3,500 purchase price. Your contract should explain whether and when the deposit is refundable or transferable.

When can puppies go home?

Go-home timing depends on the puppy's age, development, veterinary guidance, and the terms of the purchase agreement.

Do you offer a lifetime return clause?

Yes. If a family can no longer keep a Coco Crown puppy, the dog must be returned to Coco Crown Kennels rather than surrendered to a shelter.

How do I reserve a puppy?

Submit the inquiry form below. After approval and review of the puppy contract, reservation instructions can be provided.
